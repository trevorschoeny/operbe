## Statement
A person can put a conversation out of the way without deleting it. It leaves the active list at once, and for a few seconds the product offers Undo.

## Flow
1. The person chooses to archive a conversation.
2. The product removes it from the active list and says it was archived.
3. The product offers Undo for at least five seconds.

## Appears when
The person may change the conversation.

## Usable when
The conversation is not already archived. Otherwise it is shown but can't be used, with "Already archived."

## Undo
Returns the conversation to the active list, where it was.

## Examples
- **Usual.** Given an active conversation I may change, when I archive it, then it leaves the active list, appears in the archive, and Undo is offered.
- **Not allowed.** Given a conversation I may only read, then archiving isn't offered at all.
- **Wrong moment.** Given an archived conversation, then archiving is shown but can't be used, with "Already archived."
- **Offline.** Given I have no connection, when I archive it, then it leaves the active list and is marked as waiting; when I'm back it completes.
- **Refused.** Given the server refuses, then the conversation returns to the active list and I'm told why.
