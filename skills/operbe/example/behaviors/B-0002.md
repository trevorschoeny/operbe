## Statement
In any list of conversations, swiping a row fully to the left archives that conversation, exactly as Archive a conversation (B-0001) does. A partial swipe reveals an Archive button and does nothing more.

## Examples
- **Full swipe.** When I swipe a row all the way left and let go, the conversation is archived and Undo is offered.
- **Partial swipe.** When I swipe a row part of the way, an Archive button shows on the right; letting go leaves the row open with the button.
- **Not allowed.** On a conversation I may only read, the row doesn't swipe.
